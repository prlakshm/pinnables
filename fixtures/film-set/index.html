Accession 0612 · the flower of June

The Rose

ACC 0612 · sheet 1 of 3

Field notes

June's flower, and the oldest assignment in the register — the Romans gave the month to Juno and the rose to Venus, and the pairing outlasted both. The apothecary rose held here has been in continuous cultivation since the 1300s.
